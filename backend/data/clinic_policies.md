# Westside Family Health Clinic - Patient Policies & Guidelines

## 1. Appointments & Scheduling
*   **Walk-in Hours:** Walk-in patients are accepted for urgent care needs between 8:00 AM and 11:30 AM, Monday through Friday. Walk-ins are seen on a triage basis (severity of symptoms), not first-come, first-served.
*   **Cancellations & No-Shows:** We require at least 24 hours' notice for appointment cancellations. Patients who fail to show up for a scheduled appointment without notifying the clinic will be charged a $50 no-show fee. After three no-shows in a calendar year, the patient may be dismissed from the practice.
*   **Late Arrivals:** If you arrive more than 15 minutes late for your scheduled appointment, you may be asked to reschedule to ensure other patients are not delayed.

## 2. Insurance & Billing
*   **Accepted Insurances:** We currently accept Medicare, Blue Cross Blue Shield (PPO and HMO), Aetna, and UnitedHealthcare. We do *not* currently accept Medicaid or Kaiser Permanente HMO plans.
*   **Co-pays:** All insurance co-pays are due at the time of service. We accept credit cards, debit cards, and FSA/HSA cards. We do not accept cash or personal checks.
*   **Out-of-Pocket Estimates:** For uninsured patients, a standard office visit starts at $125. Additional tests (e.g., strep test, flu swab) are billed separately. A full price list is available at the front desk.

## 3. Laboratory & Testing Guidelines
*   **Fasting for Blood Tests:** 
    *   *Lipid Panels (Cholesterol):* Require a 12-hour strict fast. You may drink water and take your regular non-diabetic medications.
    *   *Comprehensive Metabolic Panel (CMP) / Fasting Glucose:* Require an 8-hour fast.
    *   *Complete Blood Count (CBC) or Thyroid (TSH):* No fasting required.
*   **Test Results:** Routine lab results are typically available in the patient portal within 3-5 business days. The doctor will only call you directly if a result is abnormal and requires immediate action. Please do not call the clinic for results before 5 business days have passed.

## 4. Prescription Refills
*   **Refill Requests:** Please request refills through your pharmacy or the online patient portal. Do not call the main clinic line for routine refills.
*   **Processing Time:** Allow up to 48 hours (2 business days) for all prescription refill requests to be processed. 
*   **Controlled Substances:** Refills for controlled substances (e.g., Adderall, Norco) require an in-person or telehealth visit every 3 months. No early refills will be provided under any circumstances.

## 5. Telehealth Requirements
*   Telehealth appointments are only available for established patients who have been seen in the clinic within the last 12 months.
*   Telehealth is appropriate for: medication follow-ups, discussing lab results, mild cold/allergy symptoms, and mental health check-ins.
*   Telehealth is *not* appropriate for: chest pain, severe abdominal pain, breathing difficulties, or new acute injuries (e.g., suspected fractures). For these, please visit an urgent care or ER.
